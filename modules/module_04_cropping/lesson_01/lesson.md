# Cropping and Composition

**Topic:** photo editing
**Objective:** Use framing to improve visual hierarchy.

## Theory
In **Cropping and Composition**, this lesson covers **Cropping and Composition**. Goal: Use framing to improve visual hierarchy.

## Example
Analyze the original image and identify regions that need exposure, tonal, or color adjustments before editing.

## Editing Breakdown
1. Start with global tonal adjustments.
2. Recover highlights and open shadows where needed.
3. Refine color and white balance.
4. Apply local edits to direct attention to the subject.

## Exercise
Recreate the target edit from the original image and compare your histogram, highlight detail, and color harmony against the target.
